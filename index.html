Getting on the trail from a tablet

Trailhead Trek runs right in the browser, so there's nothing to install — an iPad or Android tablet works exactly like a laptop does. Here's the fastest way to get your explorer set up.

1

Open the trail map

Tap the link below in Safari (iPad) or Chrome (Android tablet). The trail, stops, and stars all scale to fit the screen automatically — portrait or landscape.

2

Pin it to the home screen

From the browser's share menu, choose Add to Home Screen. It'll sit next to your other apps and open straight to the trail with one tap — no browser bar, no typing a link.

3

Pick your explorer

Choose your explorer's profile and PIN just like usual. Every tap is a simple touch, so the stops, star badges, and Parent Dashboard all work the same as on a computer.

Trail notes

Stars and progress travel with the link, not the tablet. As long as it's opened from this same link, an explorer's saved stops and stars show up whether you're on the tablet, a phone, or a laptop.

If more than one tablet will play, add the home-screen shortcut on each one — they'll all point to the same trail and stay in sync.
